Curto Serif is a refined, contemporary serif typeface designed for editorial elegance, titles, and bold brand identities. It balances sharp, high-contrast details with a calm text rhythm, giving headlines presence while remaining comfortable across longer passages.

The family spans Regular (400) through Bold (700) in Roman and Italic, with both static and variable (wght) builds. Curto Serif covers the Google Fonts Latin Core glyphset, including Latin Extended, punctuation, symbols, and currency characters needed for broad Latin-script typography. The variable fonts make it easy to tune weight continuously from 400 to 700.

Curto Serif is designed by Dev Chauhan and released under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Sources, build scripts, and release archives are available at github.com/dqev/curto.
